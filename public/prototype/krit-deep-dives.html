Hi, and thanks for reading.

You're looking at Krit Deep Dives — an early experiment in what reading-with-practice could feel like. Four prototypes, four different topics, four different ways to teach. Behind tab 02 is a 5-minute finance simulator; behind 04 is a short physics paper that proves your intuition about bicycles is wrong. The point isn't the topics — it's the format underneath them.

We're testing whether this format does what we hope: makes a busy adult actually understand something in 30 minutes, instead of skimming it. Your read is the only way we find out.

What we'd love from you

  1. Read at your pace. Tab through 01 → 04 in order if you can, or skip around. Don't feel obligated to finish all four — read what holds you, bounce where it doesn't.
  2. Notice the bounces. Where did you start skimming? Where did your eyes glaze? Where did you laugh, or stop, or go back? Those moments are gold.
  3. Spend 3 minutes on tab 05 at the end. A short survey. Your read shapes what we build next.
