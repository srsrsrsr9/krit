AI-Powered Content Pipeline

Brief, draft, review, ship — and where AI fits in each

notes by Vikrant ✏️

~ 14 min read ~

Most teams adopt AI for content the same way: turn it on, ship 10x volume, watch traffic die six months later, blame Google. The pattern has a name now — *AI-generated SEO slop* — and the search engines have been training on it for two years.

Mental model: brief → draft → review → ship
Every piece of content should pass through four stages, in order, no skipping.

Move 1 — The brand-voice anchor

  • AI defaults to a tone called *generic LinkedIn earnest*. You can spot it in three sentences: short paragraphs, lots of em-dashes, em-dashes used as commas, vague affirmations, and a closing call-to-action that says 'embrace the journey'.
  • Step 1 — Find your three voice anchors: Open the three highest-performing pieces of content from the last 12 months. Highlight the lines a customer would quote. Those are your voice anchors. Real sentences, not vibes.
  • Step 2 — Write a Do/Don't list: From those highlights, extract patterns. 'Do say: specific timeframes, ratio numbers, named customer types.' 'Don't say: aspirational verbs without a noun behind them, journey metaphors, glow.'
  • Step 3 — Attach 3 full sample paragraphs: Tell AI: 'Write in the voice of these three paragraphs.' Paste them. AI is a pattern-matcher; it pattern-matches what you give it.
  • Step 4 — Lock it in the system prompt: Don't paste the anchor into every chat. Wire it into your tooling — Notion AI, Claude project, whatever you use. The anchor should be persistent infrastructure, not a per-task copy-paste.
  • Step 5 — Audit drift monthly: Take 5 random pieces shipped this month. Compare to anchor. Score each on a 1-5 voice match. If average drops below 4, refresh the samples.

Move 2 — The 3-pass review

  • Once a draft exists, three passes — in order, by name. Each pass has one job.

Traps to flag for the exam

The classic trap: AI-generated SEO slop: The slop pattern: high volume + no voice anchor + no fact pass + 'rank-first, value-second'. Search engines have been trained on this exact signature for two years; they spot it within weeks of indexing.

Takeaways (memorize these)

AI is a draft engine, not a content factory — the pipeline is the protection.
Without a brand-voice anchor, AI defaults to 'generic LinkedIn earnest' and your content becomes interchangeable with competitors'.
The 3-pass review (voice, fact, slop — in that order) is the cheapest insurance against the SEO-slop trap.
Volume without voice + fact + slop discipline does not raise your brand; it lowers your search trust.
Three real high-performing paragraphs beat any number of style-guide adjectives when training AI on your voice.
