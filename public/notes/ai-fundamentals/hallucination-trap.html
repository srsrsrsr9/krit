The Hallucination Problem

Why LLMs confidently lie, and what to actually do about it

notes by Meera ✏️

~ 14 min read ~

Hallucination is the biggest reason 'AI features' get rolled back. The model writes a plausible-sounding citation that doesn't exist.

Mental model: why hallucination is structural, not a bug
Hallucination is not a temporary defect. It's a direct consequence of how LLMs work.

Move 1 — RAG (retrieval) as the real fix

  • **RAG = Retrieval-Augmented Generation.** The pattern: when a query needs factual grounding, *retrieve* the relevant documents first, then *pass them to the model* in the context, then *ask the model to answer using only those documents*. In practice: 1.
  • 1. User asks: Query: 'What does the latest RBI circular say about UPI merchant fees?'
  • 2. Without RAG: Model produces: 'RBI Circular Ref. No. RBI/2024-25/47 dated June 12, 2024 states that...' — fabricated reference number. Plausible. False. Bad day for whoever cites it.
  • 3. With RAG — embed: User query is embedded into a vector. Vector DB indexed against the actual RBI circulars corpus.
  • 4. With RAG — retrieve: Top 3 chunks come back: actual RBI circular text from circular #DPSS.CO.PD.No.1093/02.14.003/2024-25 dated April 17, 2024.
  • 5. With RAG — answer: Model produces: 'Per RBI Circular DPSS.CO.PD.No.1093/02.14.003/2024-25 (April 17, 2024), Section 3, the merchant discount rate for UPI transactions remains zero for transactions up to ₹2,000.' Real citation. Real text. Grounded.

Move 2 — When the cost of being wrong is too high to ship at all

  • RAG fixes a lot. It does not fix everything.

Traps to flag for the exam

The classic trap: the 'confident liar' shipped to customers: Most rolled-back AI features have the same shape: launched without RAG (or with bad retrieval), confident outputs, mostly correct, occasional egregious failure. The egregious failures get screenshotted, posted on Twitter, the team rolls back.

Takeaways (memorize these)

Hallucination is structural — a property of next-token prediction, not a bug
Confidence and truth overlap most of the time and diverge on the dangerous edges
RAG (retrieval) is the canonical fix — ground answers in real documents, force citations
In regulated domains, RAG with citations is the price of admission, not optional
Some use cases should be refused regardless of engineering effort — when cost-of-error is unbounded
