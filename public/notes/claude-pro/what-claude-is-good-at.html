What Claude Is Actually Good At

A capability map, not a marketing brochure

notes by Aanya ✏️

~ 13 min read ~

Most people use Claude the way they use Google in 2009: they type a question and hope. That works for trivia, and it fails for everything else.

Mental model: Claude is a fast junior with infinite patience
Picture the most willing junior on your team. They have read every book in the library. They have shipped nothing. They will not push back unless you ask. They will not check their work unless you tell them how. They will write 1,800 confident words on any topic, and they cannot tell when they are wrong.

Move 1: The Capability Map

  • Anthropic ships three surfaces in 2026: **Claude.ai** (web chat, with Projects and Artifacts), **Claude Code** (CLI in your terminal with file and shell access), and the **Anthropic API** (raw access for your own software). Three models live on each: **Opus 4.7** with the 1M-token context window for hard reasoning, **Sonnet 4.6** as the best-cost workhorse, and **Haiku 4.5** for cheap, fast, repetitive jobs.
  • Step 1: Identify the verb. Are you thinking, changing files, or running the same thing many times?
  • Step 2: Place on the column. Thinking goes to Claude.ai. Files go to Claude Code. Scale goes to the API.
  • Step 3: Pick the model. Opus only when reasoning load is real. Sonnet by default. Haiku when volume is the constraint.
  • Step 4: Decide the eval. How will you know this answer was right? If the answer is 'I will eyeball it', you have to read every word.
  • Step 5: Open the right surface. Resist the urge to start in the chat just because it is open.

Move 2: Don't Ask, Show

  • There are two ways to start a Claude conversation. The bad one: *'write me a marketing plan for a Bengaluru fintech going into Tier 2 cities'*.

Traps to flag for the exam

The classic trap: confidence without grounding: When Claude has no source, it does not say 'I don't know'. It pattern-matches to plausible-sounding text. This is the **fabrication failure**, and it is the single largest risk in your first month using Claude. Defenses: turn on web search for current facts, attach source files for domain claims, use Projects to pin a knowledge base, and assume any specific number, citation or proper name in Claude output is wrong until you check.

Takeaways (memorize these)

Claude is a fast junior with infinite patience, no memory, and a willingness to invent — hold this picture.
The capability map is one rule: Claude.ai for thinking, Claude Code for files, API for scale.
Pick the verb first, then the surface, then the model. Default Sonnet; escalate or downshift only with reason.
Don't ask, show: Claude critiquing your draft beats Claude inventing from blank.
Any specific number, citation, or proper name in Claude output is wrong until you check it.
